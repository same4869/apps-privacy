核心承诺

Lumen 是纯本地应用. 你的所有情绪数据 (星火、低语、时间胶囊、心海观察结果) 都只存在于你的 iPhone 上. 我们没有服务器. 我们从不接收、上传、或转发这些数据到任何地方.

  • 不收集任何个人数据 — 姓名、邮箱、电话、位置、联系人、通话记录、Apple ID, 一律不收集.
  • 不上传你的情绪记录 — 每一颗星火 (mood、POW 数据、低语文本、恒星归属) 都只存在于你的 iPhone 本地 UserDefaults 与 Keychain 中.
  • 不共享任何数据 — 我们没有第三方分析、没有广告 SDK、没有崩溃报告服务、没有 A/B 测试平台.
  • 不跟踪你 — 我们不使用 IDFA、不做跨 App 追踪、不请求 App Tracking Transparency 权限.
  • 无账号 — Lumen 不需要注册, 不需要登录, 不知道你是谁.

Lumen 使用的手机权限

陀螺仪 (Motion & Fitness)

仅在你双手压屏做仪式时读取, 用于计算"呼吸稳定度". 采样在仪式结束时即弃, 不写入任何持久化存储, 也从不离开你的 iPhone. 你可以在系统"设置 → 隐私 → 运动与健身 → Lumen"里关闭.

本地通知 (Notifications)

用于五种回眸推送:

  • 周年彩蛋: 投出星火后的 7 / 30 / 365 天各一次静音提醒
  • 每日回眸: 默认关闭, 用户主动开启后每晚 21:00
  • 年度回忆录: 每年 12 月 31 日 20:00
  • 时间胶囊到期: 用户封存的胶囊到达日期时
  • 心海观察 (PRO): 每周日 21:30

全部推送在本地调度, 不依赖任何服务器. 你可以在系统"设置 → Lumen → 通知"里随时关闭.

Lumen 不使用位置、相机、麦克风、照片图库、通讯录、日历、健康、蓝牙、Wi-Fi 网络列表、面容 ID、指纹、剪贴板等任何其他权限.

数据存储与保留

Lumen 的所有数据都存储在你的 iPhone 本地:

  • UserDefaults: 星火数组、封存 ID、语言偏好、主题偏好、通知开关状态
  • Keychain: "是否已购买 PRO" 的布尔值 (不含 Apple ID)
  • App 沙盒临时目录: 分享图片和导出的 JSON, 在 iOS 系统清理临时目录时自动删除

数据保留期限: 你安装 App 期间数据一直保留. 你随时可以:

  • 在 App 内"设置 → 数据 → 导出星海"备份为 JSON 文件带走
  • 直接卸载 Lumen — 所有数据随 App 一起被 iOS 系统删除, 不可恢复

Core Promise

Lumen is a fully on-device app. All your emotional data (embers, whispers, time capsules, sea insights) exists only on your iPhone. We have no servers. We never receive, upload, or forward any of it anywhere.

  • No personal data collected — no name, email, phone, location, contacts, call history, or Apple ID.
  • No emotional records uploaded — every ember (mood, POW data, whisper, star anchor) lives only in your iPhone's local UserDefaults and Keychain.
  • No data shared — no third-party analytics, no ad SDKs, no crash reporting, no A/B testing platforms.
  • No tracking — no IDFA, no cross-app tracking, no App Tracking Transparency prompt.
  • No account — Lumen requires no signup, no login. We don't know who you are.

Permissions Used

Motion & Fitness

Read only while your fingers are pressing the screen during a breathing ritual, to calculate a "breath stability" score. Samples are discarded the moment the ritual ends and never written to persistent storage or leave your iPhone. You can revoke it anytime under Settings → Privacy → Motion & Fitness → Lumen.

Notifications

Used for five types of quiet local reminders:

  • Anniversary: One silent reminder at T+7 / 30 / 365 days after placing an ember
  • Nightly Echo: Off by default; opt-in schedules 9 PM daily
  • Year in Stars: December 31 at 8 PM
  • Time Capsule: When a sealed capsule reaches its unlock date
  • Sea Insight (PRO): Sunday 9:30 PM

All notifications are scheduled locally with no server involvement. You can disable them anytime under Settings → Lumen → Notifications.

Lumen does not use Location, Camera, Microphone, Photos, Contacts, Calendar, Health, Bluetooth, Wi-Fi network list, Face ID, Touch ID, Clipboard, or any other permissions.

Data Storage & Retention

All Lumen data is stored locally on your iPhone:

  • UserDefaults: Ember array, sealed IDs, language preference, theme preference, notification toggles
  • Keychain: A single boolean for "PRO unlocked" (no Apple ID stored)
  • App Sandbox Tmp Directory: Share images and exported JSON, auto-deleted by iOS when it cleans tmp

Retention: Data persists as long as Lumen is installed. You can:

  • Back up as JSON via Settings → Data → Export Starfield
  • Uninstall Lumen — all data is deleted with the app by iOS, unrecoverable

Purchases

Lumen uses Apple StoreKit for purchases. Apple records your transactions per the App Store standard mechanism (subject to Apple's Privacy Policy), but Lumen itself does not know your Apple ID and receives no account information. We only cache a single "PRO unlocked" boolean in local Keychain.

Layer 1 one-time purchase and Layer 2 yearly keepsake are both non-subscription, non-renewing. You can restore purchases anytime (via Apple ID Family Sharing).

Your Rights (GDPR / CCPA)

Because Lumen does not collect data, most traditional privacy rights are naturally satisfied:

  • Right to be informed: This document; use of the app implies consent (no checkbox needed).
  • Right of access: Your data is on your phone — open Lumen to see it. "Export Starfield" generates a complete JSON.
  • Right to rectification: Edit whisper in ember detail (v1.1+); time capsules can be unsealed early.
  • Right to erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"): Uninstall the app to delete everything. We have no server backups.
  • Right to data portability: "Export Starfield" outputs standard JSON, importable back to Lumen or future compatible tools.
  • Right to opt out of tracking (CCPA "Do Not Sell"): Lumen does not sell / share / track. No request needed.
  • Right to lodge a complaint: If you believe we have violated your privacy, email same4869@163.com. You may also file a complaint with your local data protection authority (EU member state DPAs, California AG's office, etc.).

Children (COPPA / GDPR-K)

Lumen is not directed at children under 13 (US COPPA) or under 16 (EU GDPR). We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you are a parent or guardian and discover your child is using Lumen, please uninstall the app; all data will be deleted.

Data Security

Because all data is stored locally, Lumen's data security is equivalent to your iPhone's own security (device passcode · biometrics · Face ID / Touch ID · system-level encryption). We recommend setting a strong device passcode.

The PRO status boolean in Keychain uses kSecAttrAccessibleAfterFirstUnlockThisDeviceOnly, which is excluded from iCloud backups. Restoring to a new device does not falsely show PRO — "Restore Purchase" via Apple ID Family Sharing is needed to unlock again.

Third-Party Services

Lumen only uses Apple's first-party frameworks:

  • SwiftUI · SpriteKit · UIKit — system UI
  • CoreMotion — gyroscope sampling
  • NaturalLanguage — on-device tokenization and sentiment analysis of your whispers (fully offline)
  • StoreKit — purchases
  • UserNotifications — local notifications

No third-party SDKs integrated — no Firebase, no Sentry, no Mixpanel, no Google Analytics, no ad SDKs.
