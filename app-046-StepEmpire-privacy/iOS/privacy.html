步数王朝 隐私政策

更新日期：2026 年 7 月 31 日 · 开发者：xunwang

欢迎使用「步数王朝」（以下简称"本应用"）。本应用由独立开发者提供，重视你的隐私与数据安全。本隐私政策说明本应用如何收集、使用、存储与保护你的信息。

1. 我们收集的信息

  • 健康数据（步数）：本应用需要读取你的每日步数，用于将步数转化为游戏内的动能与资源。此数据来源于 iOS 系统 HealthKit，本应用只读取步数计数，不读取心率、体重、位置等其他健康数据，也不向 HealthKit 写入任何数据。
  • 你在应用内创建的帝国、里程碑、皇帝、章纪、成就等游戏内容。
  • 设备与应用使用信息（例如崩溃信息、基本性能统计），仅用于提升稳定性与体验。
  • Game Center 相关信息：若你登录 Game Center，Apple 会将你的成就与排行榜数据同步到 Game Center 服务。本应用不接触你的 Apple ID 或 Game Center 用户名，只上传成就与排行榜的匿名数值。

2. 信息使用

  • 用于提供核心功能：步数转化为游戏内动能、里程碑推进、每日政事、皇帝养成等。
  • 用于将成就与排行榜（累计步履、名声峰值、宫阙营造）通过 Game Center 展示给你。
  • 用于改进产品体验与保障安全。
  • 用于内购状态管理（Pro · 凌烟至臻），付款流程由 Apple App Store 处理，本应用不接触你的支付信息。

3. 信息共享

  • 我们不会出售你的个人信息。
  • 不会与任何第三方广告或分析平台共享数据。
  • 仅在以下情况下共享：你主动分享（如截图分享到社交媒体）；法律法规要求；使用 Apple 提供的服务（HealthKit、Game Center、In-App Purchase）时按 Apple 的隐私政策处理。

4. 数据存储与安全

  • 你的游戏进度、皇帝设定、装扮偏好等数据全部存储在本地设备上（SwiftData）。
  • 本应用不设置任何云端服务器，你的游戏数据不会上传到开发者控制的任何位置。
  • Game Center 的成就与排行榜数据存储在 Apple 服务器，由 Apple 依据其隐私政策管理。
  • 本应用使用 iOS 数据保护机制与系统级安全能力。

5. HealthKit 权限

  • 首次启动时，本应用会请求访问你的步数数据。你可以拒绝此权限，但此时本应用无法计步，游戏内的资源无法产出。
  • 你可以随时在 iOS 系统「设置 → 健康 → 数据访问和设备 → 步数王朝」中关闭本应用的健康数据访问。
  • 本应用只使用只读权限（HKQuantityType.stepCount），不请求任何写入权限。

6. In-App Purchase · Pro · 凌烟至臻

  • 本应用提供一项一次性内购（Pro · 凌烟至臻，¥98），支持家庭共享。
  • 付款流程完全由 Apple App Store 处理，本应用不接触你的 Apple ID、银行卡、支付密码等任何支付信息。
  • 购买后可通过应用内的「恢复购买」在同一 Apple ID 的其他设备上恢复权益。
  • 如需退款，请通过 Apple 的官方渠道申请：报告问题（reportaproblem.apple.com）。

7. 数据保留与删除

  • 你可以随时在应用内的「陛下 → 设置」中重置全部数据。
  • 删除应用会永久删除本地保存的所有游戏数据。
  • Game Center 上传的成就与排行榜数据由 Apple 管理，可在 Game Center 设置中管理。

8. 你的权利

  • 你可以随时访问、修改或删除应用内的游戏数据。
  • 你可以在 iOS 系统设置中撤销 HealthKit 或 Game Center 权限。
  • 如需帮助，请通过下方邮箱联系开发者。

9. 未成年人

如你未达到你所在地区的法定年龄，请在监护人同意与指导下使用本应用。本应用适合 4 岁以上用户，无暴力、赌博、成人内容。

10. 更新

Step Empire · Privacy Policy (English)

Last updated: July 31, 2026 · Developer: xunwang

Welcome to Step Empire ("the App"). The App is provided by an independent developer and values your privacy and data security. This policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your information.

1. Information We Collect

  • Health data (step count): The App reads your daily step count from HealthKit to convert steps into in-game kinesis and resources. We only read step count, not heart rate, weight, location, or any other health data, and we never write to HealthKit.
  • Your empire progress, milestones, emperors, chronicle, and achievements created within the App.
  • Basic device and app usage information (crash logs, performance metrics) to improve stability.
  • Game Center information: if you sign in to Game Center, Apple syncs your achievements and leaderboard scores to Game Center. The App never touches your Apple ID or Game Center username — only anonymous achievement and score values are uploaded.

2. How We Use Information

  • To provide core features: converting steps into kinesis, milestone progression, daily affairs, emperor customization.
  • To display achievements and leaderboards (Total Steps, Peak Fame, Structures Raised) via Game Center.
  • To improve product quality and security.
  • To manage in-app purchase state (Pro · Lingyan Peak). Payment is handled by Apple App Store; the App never touches your payment information.

3. Sharing

  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not share data with any third-party advertising or analytics platform.
  • We share information only when you choose to share (e.g., screenshots to social media), when required by law, or through Apple-provided services (HealthKit, Game Center, In-App Purchase), governed by Apple's privacy policies.

4. Storage & Security

  • Your game progress, emperor choice, cosmetic preferences, and other data are stored locally on your device (via SwiftData).
  • The App has no developer-controlled server. Your game data is never uploaded to any location controlled by the developer.
  • Game Center achievement and leaderboard data is stored on Apple's servers, managed under Apple's privacy policy.
  • The App uses iOS data protection and system-level security.

5. HealthKit Permission

  • On first launch the App requests access to your step count. You may deny this — but without it, the App cannot count steps and no in-game resources can be produced.
  • You can revoke access at any time in iOS Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices → Step Empire.
  • The App uses read-only permission (HKQuantityType.stepCount) and never requests write access.

6. In-App Purchase · Pro · Lingyan Peak

  • The App offers a single one-time in-app purchase (Pro · Lingyan Peak, $14.99 tier), with Family Sharing enabled.
  • Payment is fully handled by the Apple App Store. The App never touches your Apple ID, credit card, payment password, or any payment credentials.
  • After purchase, use the "Restore Purchase" button in-app to recover the entitlement on other devices signed into the same Apple ID.
  • For refunds, use Apple's official refund process at reportaproblem.apple.com.

7. Data Retention & Deletion

  • You can reset all data any time in Sovereign → Settings within the App.
  • Uninstalling the App permanently deletes all locally stored game data.
  • Game Center data (achievements and leaderboards) is managed by Apple and can be managed via your Game Center settings.

8. Your Rights

  • You can access, modify, or delete your in-app game data at any time.
  • You can revoke HealthKit or Game Center permissions in iOS Settings.
  • Contact the developer at the email below for help.

9. Minors

If you are under the legal age in your region, please use the App with the guidance and consent of a guardian. The App is rated 4+ and contains no violence, gambling, or adult content.

10. Changes

We may update this policy and will indicate the update date on this page. Material changes will be surfaced in-app.
